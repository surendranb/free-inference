### 2. Provider Spotlight: SambaNova Systems
SambaNova remains a core high-performance provider within the free inference ecosystem, leveraging its Reconfigurable Dataflow Architecture (RDA) to deliver high-throughput, low-latency execution for large language models without financial cost.

- **Base URL**: `https://api.sambanova.ai/v1`
- **Authentication**: Bearer token via `Authorization: Header` (requires a free account registration at sambanova.ai).
- **API Compatibility**: Fully OpenAI-compatible endpoints (`/chat/completions`, `/models`).
- **Standard Free Limits**: Geared toward rapid prototyping and development workloads, featuring generous burst capacity for models such as Llama 3 and associated open-weight variants.
- **Recommended Use Case**: Ideal for streaming applications and high-concurrency evaluation scripts where time-to-first-token (TTFT) is critical.

### 3. Weekly Recipe

